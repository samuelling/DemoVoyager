    SAMPLES · 8-BIT · 22 kHz

      Every pixel is computed in software into a 320×200 buffer and stretched to 4:3, the way a VGA monitor showed mode 13h. The music is a six-channel tracker module: 64-row patterns of notes and hex effect commands (0xy arpeggio, 4xy vibrato, Cxx volume), played by 8-bit samples synthesized when you press play. The echo channel is just the lead copied three rows late at lower volume, the old tracker trick.
